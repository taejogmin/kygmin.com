blueberry-smoothiePLAY ►

Say a Korean sentence and watch your own pitch drawn as a curve, in semitones relative to your own median. YIN pitch tracking, syllable-nucleus detection and a Viterbi octave correction run entirely inside the page — read five sentences chosen so that only the sentence type differs, or just talk freely for ninety seconds. It is a measuring instrument, not a dialect quiz: it never tells you where you are from. Your voice never leaves your device and nothing is stored.

vocal-rangePLAY ►

How high and how low can you actually hold a note — not touch, hold? Two short recordings, no musical training required: you are never told to sing a particular pitch, only to go a little lower than before, and the measurement is allowed to break before you have to decide where your limit is. The result is two ranges rather than one, because a note you can reach once cannot carry a song, and then the only number a karaoke machine understands: how many times to press the key button. A blueberry-smoothie spin-off. Nothing is stored.

pyrrhus-coPLAY ►

Twenty rounds of sealed first-price bidding, three rivals, and the same lots on the block. Nobody knows what anything is really worth — you get an estimate, and you can pay to look closer. Bid too little and you go home empty-handed; bid what it feels worth and you have already paid too much. The house is always happy to buy a lot back, at a price of its own choosing.

indian-pokerPLAY ►

The one card you cannot see is your own. Their card is face up in front of you and yours is face up in front of them, so every bet they make is a statement about your hand — read them, and try not to be readable. Twenty-seven rounds off one split deck, three sources of unequal honesty for sale, and four desks to sit across from. The last one keeps learning you.
