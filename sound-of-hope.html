CHARITY CONCERT — KOREAN YOUTH ASSOCIATION IN CAMBODIA

SOUND OF HOPE

A charity concert series in Phnom Penh, performed by Korean expatriate youth alongside local Cambodian students, raising money for children's medical care. I led the second concert — securing corporate sponsors, recruiting performers, promoting the event, and performing flute on the night.

28 JAN 2017 (2ND CONCERT) · PPIIA CONCERT HALL, PHNOM PENH · ROLE: PROJECT LEAD & PERFORMER

The Concert

"Sound of Hope for the Cambodian Children" is a charity concert organised by the Korean Youth Association in Cambodia, pairing Korean expatriate students with local Cambodian students for a joint evening of music in Phnom Penh. The first concert, in 2016, raised enough to help sponsor Preah Ang Duong Hospital. Building on that, we staged a second concert on 28 January 2017 at the PPIIA Concert Hall, this time to fund cataract surgeries for two Cambodian children who needed the operations but couldn't afford them.

2nd annual concert Phnom Penh, Cambodia Korean & Cambodian students Medical fundraiser

My Role — Project Lead

I led the 2017 concert end to end: pitching and securing sponsorship from local companies and financial institutions to cover the venue and running costs, recruiting the student performers on both the Korean and Cambodian sides, and promoting the event so the hall would actually fill up. I also performed — flute, alongside the local Cambodian students I'd spent six months rehearsing with.

Impact

The concert raised $4,100, donated to Angduong National Eye Hospital to fund eye surgeries for two underprivileged Cambodian children. It followed the first concert's donation the year before, which helped sponsor Preah Ang Duong Hospital in Cambodia.
