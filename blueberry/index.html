SPIN-OFF

vocal-rangeOPEN ►

The pitch tracker on this page, pointed at singing instead of speech. It measures how high and how low you can hold a note — hold, not touch — and turns your top note into the one number a karaoke machine understands: how many times to press the key button. It is a separate page, not a third tab here: the octave correction and phrase splitting that make this page work would wreck that one, since it needs you to sweep two octaves on purpose.

v0 deliberately gives no dialect verdict. The literature can support one claim — whether a speaker rises sharply at the end of a yes/no question — and even that needs a validated baseline this page does not yet have. Until then it only shows you the measurement.
