MICROPHONE OFF

Your voice is analysed entirely inside this page. No audio is uploaded, no recording is saved to disk, and this version stores no results at all — closing the tab erases everything. The microphone is only open while the indicator above is red.

What is measured (full list)
  • f0 per 10 ms frame → semitones relative to your own median
  • Syllable nuclei and boundaries from the energy envelope
  • Phrase boundaries from pauses (≥200 ms)
  • Pitch movement over the last two targets of each phrase
  • Pitch range, variability, speech rate, pause structure
  • No words are transcribed. No speech recognition runs anywhere.
