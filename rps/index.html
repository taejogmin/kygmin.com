How the AI Works

Three Markov predictors run in parallel: one conditioned on your last move, one on your last two moves, and one on the last round's result — the strongest human tells live in how you react to a win or a loss. Whichever predictor has been most accurate lately gets to make the call. Old observations decay, so if you change your strategy the AI follows. When its best guess isn't confident enough, it plays random on purpose so you can't reverse-read it.

Fairness — commit & reveal: before you pick, the AI locks its move and shows you a SHA-256 hash of it (top-right of the board). After the round, the move and the random nonce are revealed so you can verify the hash matches. The AI physically cannot see your move and then choose.

The habit report unlocks only when the evidence is real: at least 30 decided rounds, p < 0.01 on a one-sided test against 50%, held for 5 consecutive rounds. Individual habits are shown only if they pass their own significance test with enough observations.

Privacy: your play data is just move counts — no personal data. It lives in your browser (localStorage). If you sign in, it also syncs to your account so you can continue on any device.
