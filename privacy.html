KYGMIN.COM — HOW YOUR DATA IS HANDLED

PRIVACY POLICY

KYGMIN.COM is a personal website and playground run by an individual, Kyung Min Park. It collects as little as technically possible, and this page explains — plainly — exactly what is and isn't collected.

EFFECTIVE: 29 JUL 2026 · CONTACT: TAEJO129129@GMAIL.COM

The Short Version

No ads, no ad trackers, no selling of data — ever. Your camera photos never leave your browser. Games work fully without an account; if you choose to sign in, only your login identity, a nickname you pick, and anonymous game statistics (like rock/paper/scissors move counts) are stored.

What Each Part of the Site Handles

ANIMAL LOOKALIKECamera and photo analysis runs 100% inside your browser (on-device machine learning). Photos are never uploaded, never stored, and never seen by anyone — the server receives nothing.
RPS PREDICTORYour play pattern (counts of rock/paper/scissors choices and outcomes) is stored in your own browser (localStorage). If you sign in, that same anonymous statistical model is also saved to your account so you can continue on another device. No photos, no free text, nothing else.
NINE POSITIONSThe game runs in your browser. To improve the AI, anonymous gameplay records are saved (cards played, declarations, results) — tied to your account if signed in, or to a random anonymous ID otherwise. No personal information is included.
WHO WROTE THISThe chat export you attach is read, modelled and scored entirely inside your browser. The file is never uploaded, the messages are never stored, and the verdicts are not stored either — closing the tab discards everything. (The sign-in widget at the top of the page is a site-wide element and has nothing to do with your chat data.)
OTHER GAMESOther playground pages run entirely in your browser and store nothing on any server.
ACCOUNTSSigning in is optional. If you sign in with email, Kakao, or Facebook, the authentication service stores your account identifier, email address (when provided by the login method), sign-in timestamps, and the nickname you choose. Nicknames are publicly visible (e.g. on share cards).

Third-Party Services

This site relies on a small number of infrastructure providers. GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) per GitHub's own privacy policy. Supabase provides authentication and database storage; account and game data is stored in Supabase's Seoul (South Korea) region. Kakao and Facebook (Meta) act only as login providers when you choose to use them — the site receives only your basic account identity from them, never your friend lists, posts, or messages. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Cookies & Local Storage

The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Browser storage is used for two things only: keeping you signed in (authentication session tokens) and saving game progress locally. Clearing your browser's site data removes both.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can use everything on this site without an account. If you have an account and want it — and all data tied to it — deleted, email taejo129129@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or with your nickname), and it will be removed, typically within 7 days. You may also request a copy of the data held about you.

Facebook data deletion If you signed in with Facebook and want your data deleted, the same applies: email taejo129129@gmail.com and your account and all associated data will be deleted. This page serves as the data deletion instructions for the Facebook Login integration.

Children

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you believe a child has created an account, contact the email above and it will be deleted.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the effective date at the top will be updated. Since the site is open source, the full history of this page is publicly visible on GitHub.
